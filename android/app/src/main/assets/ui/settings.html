App settings

Sign in to a site here, on its own page, and the share menu reads posts the way you see them: private accounts, full carousels, reels. Curio never sees these passwords; the sign-in stays on this phone.
Nothing is posted or shared to these sites. Curio reads only the post you share, to turn it into a reference on your board, and takes nothing else from your account.
Disconnecting forgets the app password on this phone. Revoke it in Nextcloud under Security, Devices & sessions.
