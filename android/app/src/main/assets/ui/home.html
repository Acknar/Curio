Use an app password instead

Create one in Nextcloud under Personal settings → Security → Devices & sessions → Create new app password.

Clip from any app

  1. In Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, X, Reddit, YouTube or your browser, tap Share.
  2. Choose Add to Curio (pin it to the top of the share menu for one-tap access).
  3. Check the title, description, place and tags, pick a board, then Add to board.

Photos, videos and PDFs shared from your gallery or files are uploaded as they are. Selected text can be saved as a note from the text selection menu.
