Curio turns the images, links, videos and notes you collect into visual boards you can filter, tag and share.

Paste a link from Pinterest, Instagram, YouTube or any website and it pulls in the picture, title and a snapshot for you. Upload your own photos, drop in a PDF, or jot a quick note.

On your phone, you can also add from the share menu of any app. The next screens show how.

  • In Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, your browser or any other app, tap Share, then Add to Curio.
  • On most phones you can long-press Add to Curio in the share menu and pin it, so it stays first.
  • Check the title, description, tags and board, then tap Add to board. The reference is in Curio straight away.
  • Photos, videos and PDFs from your gallery or Files app, and text you select, are shared the same way.

Hashtags in a caption become tag suggestions, and a place named in it is put on the map.

Some sites show more to people who are signed in. Sign in here once and the share menu reads posts the way you see them: private accounts, full carousels, reels. This is optional.

Nothing is posted or shared to these sites. Curio reads only the post you share, to turn it into a reference on your board, and takes nothing else from your account.

Curio never sees these passwords; the sign-in stays on this phone. You can change this later in App settings.
