Curio Web Clipper

Connect the clipper to your Nextcloud to add posts, images, videos and pages to your boards from any website.

The clipper needs access to websites to read the page you clip and to reach your Nextcloud.
Finish signing in on the page that opened, then come back here.
or
Create one in Nextcloud under Personal settings, Security, Devices & sessions.
Disconnecting forgets the app password in this browser. Revoke it in Nextcloud under Security, Devices & sessions.
Hold the key and click an image, a video, a PDF link or selected text to clip it.
    The clipper reads the page in your browser as you see it, including posts on Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok or X when you are signed in there. What you clip goes to your Nextcloud and nowhere else. Nothing is posted to those sites and nothing else is taken from your accounts.
